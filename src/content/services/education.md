Teaching is where most of what we know started. Years of writing tutorials, running workshops and answering questions in the Laravel community taught us that the best training is built around the work people actually have to do, not around what makes a tidy slide deck.

We maintain the learning pathways for the official Laravel Certification, and that same material shapes the workshops and mentoring we run for teams.

Every programme starts with your codebase and your team. The goal is something specific that's different afterwards: a practice adopted, a pattern understood, a developer ready to lead.
