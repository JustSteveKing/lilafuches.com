Most of the companies we work with don't need another pair of hands. They need someone senior enough to look at the whole system, the code, the team and the roadmap, and say what to do next, and then stay around long enough to see it through.

That's what this side of the work is. Steve has been a CTO, an engineering manager and a fractional CTO through more than one acquisition, and the advice comes from having made those calls before rather than from a framework on a slide.

When it's time to build, we build. A lot of our engineering work is Laravel and API work, done to the conventions your team already knows, because the measure of a good engagement is how easily your own people can carry on once it ends.
